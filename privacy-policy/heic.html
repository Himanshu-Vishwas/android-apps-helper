Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when You use the HEIC Viewer & Converter mobile application.

HEIC Viewer & Converter is designed to work primarily offline. Image viewing and conversion are performed locally on Your device. We respect Your privacy and strive to collect as little information as possible.

Interpretation and Definitions

Definitions

  • Application means the HEIC Viewer & Converter mobile application provided by Marsman.
  • Company refers to Marsman ("We", "Us", "Our").
  • Country refers to India.
  • Device means any Android phone, tablet, Chromebook, or other compatible device.
  • Personal Data means information relating to an identified or identifiable person.
  • Service means the HEIC Viewer & Converter application.
  • Usage Data means diagnostic and analytics information automatically collected while using the application.
  • You means the individual using the application.

Information We Collect

Photos and Files

HEIC Viewer & Converter allows You to open, view, and convert image files stored on Your device.

Your photos and files are processed locally on Your device. We do not upload Your images or converted files to Our servers.

Device Permissions

The application may request permission to:

  • Access Photos and Videos
  • Access Files and Documents
  • Save converted images

These permissions are required only to open files selected by You and save converted images.

Usage Data

If analytics or crash reporting is enabled, anonymous diagnostic information such as device model, Android version, app version, and crash logs may be collected to improve app stability.

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide image viewing and conversion features.
  • Improve application performance and stability.
  • Fix crashes and software bugs.
  • Improve compatibility with Android devices.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Local Processing

HEIC Viewer & Converter performs image decoding and conversion directly on Your device.

  • No image uploads.
  • No cloud processing.
  • No remote storage.
  • No account required.
  • No sign-in required.

Advertising

If the application displays advertisements, Google AdMob may collect certain information to provide and measure advertisements.

Learn more: Google's Privacy Policy

Analytics and Crash Reporting

We may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand application performance and improve reliability.

Firebase may collect anonymous diagnostic information including device model, operating system version, app version, crash reports, and usage statistics.

More information: Firebase Privacy

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade Your personal information.

Your images remain under Your control unless You explicitly share them using Android's built-in sharing features.

Data Retention

Since image processing occurs locally, We generally do not retain Your images.

Diagnostic and analytics information, if collected, is retained according to Google Firebase policies.

Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

  • Grant or revoke permissions through Android Settings.
  • Delete converted files at any time.
  • Uninstall the application to stop all future data collection.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect Your information. Since processing is primarily performed on Your device, Your files remain under Your control.
