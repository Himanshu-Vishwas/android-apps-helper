Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Mars Launcher ("the App") is designed to provide a fast, lightweight and privacy-focused home screen experience. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data.

Information We Collect

Mars Launcher does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or payment information.

The App does not use analytics services, advertising services, or cloud-based tracking.

Installed Applications

Mars Launcher accesses the list of installed applications on your device solely to display them on the home screen and allow you to launch them.

This information remains on your device and is never transmitted to us or stored on our servers.

Permissions

Mars Launcher only requests permissions that are necessary to provide launcher functionality.

Permissions are used only for their intended purpose and only when required by the features you choose to use.

Advertising

Mars Launcher does not display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

Analytics

Mars Launcher does not use analytics or crash reporting services.

Data Security

Your launcher preferences and app information remain on your device. While we strive to build a secure application, no software can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Mars Launcher is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
