Privacy Policy for One More Turn

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Marsman ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile game One More Turn (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and processed when you use the Game.

By downloading or using One More Turn, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

One More Turn is designed as an offline-first game. You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to play the core game.

Information Stored on Your Device

The Game may store game-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Level progress
  • Stars and scores
  • Best move counts
  • Game settings
  • Sound and music preferences
  • Haptic preferences
  • Achievements
  • Daily challenge progress
  • Game statistics
  • Cosmetic unlocks, if applicable
  • Purchase status

This information is used to provide game functionality and is primarily stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising

One More Turn may display advertisements through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.

AdMob may collect and process certain information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location information, and information related to advertising interactions, subject to Google's policies and applicable law.

The Game may use different types of advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. For example, you may voluntarily watch an advertisement to receive an in-game reward such as additional undo moves or a hint.

We do not intentionally collect personal information through advertisements.

Google's processing of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Advertising Consent and Preferences

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, the Game may provide a consent or privacy-options interface before personalized advertising is displayed.

Where required, advertising preferences will be handled in accordance with applicable laws and Google's advertising requirements.

You may also be able to manage advertising preferences through your Android device or Google account settings.

4. In-App Purchases

One More Turn may offer optional in-app purchases, such as a possible "Remove Ads" purchase.

Payments are processed through Google Play. We do not directly receive or store your credit card, debit card, bank account, or other payment credentials.

Google Play processes purchase information according to its own terms and privacy policies.

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

We may use analytics or diagnostic services to understand general game performance and improve One More Turn.

Depending on the services enabled in the released version, this information may include:

  • Game launches
  • Level starts and completions
  • Level failures
  • Feature usage
  • Ad interactions
  • Performance information
  • Crash and diagnostic information

Analytics and diagnostic services, when enabled, will be configured in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

6. Internet Connectivity

The core gameplay of One More Turn is designed to work without an internet connection.

An internet connection may be required for optional services such as:

  • Loading advertisements
  • Processing in-app purchases
  • Optional analytics
  • Crash or diagnostic services
  • Other third-party services used by the Game

If an internet connection is unavailable, core offline gameplay should continue where possible.

7. Children's Privacy

One More Turn does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to play the core game.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through account registration or similar mechanisms.

Third-party services used by the Game, including advertising services, may have their own policies and requirements concerning children and age-restricted users.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us by a child in a manner that requires removal, please contact us using the contact information below.

8. Third-Party Services

One More Turn may use third-party services to provide advertising, payments, analytics, diagnostics, or other functionality.

Depending on the released version of the Game, these services may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play Services
  • Analytics or crash-reporting services, if enabled

Third-party services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the Game.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Game progress and preferences stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, uninstall the Game, or clear the Game's application data.

Information processed by third-party services is retained according to the policies and retention practices of those services.

11. Your Choices

Depending on your device, location, and applicable law, you may have choices regarding:

  • Advertising personalization
  • Consent preferences
  • Device advertising identifiers
  • Analytics preferences, where applicable
  • Notifications

You may also uninstall the Game or clear its local application data through your device settings.
