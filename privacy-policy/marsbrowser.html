Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Mars Browser ("the App") is designed to provide a fast, lightweight and privacy-focused web browsing experience. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data.

Information We Collect

Mars Browser does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or payment information.

The App does not use analytics services, advertising services, or cloud-based tracking.

Browsing Data

Your browsing history, cookies, cache, downloads, and other browsing data are stored locally on your device to provide browser functionality.

This information is not transmitted to us and is never stored on our servers.

Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Mars Browser may request certain Android permissions, including:

  • Internet – To access websites.
  • Storage / Media Access – To save and manage downloaded files.
  • Camera – Only when a website requests camera access or when using QR code scanning (if available).
  • Microphone – Only when a website requests microphone access.
  • Location – Only if you grant permission and a website requests your location.

Permissions are used only for their intended purpose and only when required by the features you choose to use.

Cookies and Website Data

Websites you visit may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies. Mars Browser provides controls to clear cookies, cache and other browsing data through the Settings menu.

Third-Party Websites

When you visit websites through Mars Browser, those websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the content, privacy practices or services provided by third-party websites.

Advertising

Mars Browser does not display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

Analytics

Mars Browser does not use analytics or crash reporting services.

Data Security

Your browsing data remains on your device unless you choose to share or upload it through websites you visit. While we strive to build a secure application, no software can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Mars Browser is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
