Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AR Measure ("the Application") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the application.

AR Measure is developed by Marsman and provides augmented reality (AR) measurement functionality, including distance, height, width, area, and room measurements.

Definitions

  • Application refers to AR Measure.
  • Company refers to Marsman.
  • Device refers to any supported Android device.
  • Service refers to the AR Measure application.
  • Personal Data means information relating to an identifiable person.
  • Usage Data means technical and diagnostic information collected automatically.

Information We Collect

Camera Access

The application requires camera access to provide augmented reality measurement functionality.

Camera images are processed locally on your device to detect surfaces, track movement, and perform measurements.

We do not store or upload your camera feed to our servers.

Storage Access

The application may request access to storage so that screenshots and measurement records can be saved on your device.

Sensor Information

The application may access sensors such as the accelerometer, gyroscope, and depth sensors to improve measurement accuracy.

Measurement Data

Measurements generated within the application may be stored locally on your device.

Firebase Services

We may use Firebase services, including the following:

  • Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging

Firestore may be used to retrieve application data, configuration information, announcements, measurement tips, or other content.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may collect anonymous information, including:

  • Device model
  • Android version
  • Application version
  • Crash reports
  • Performance information
  • General usage statistics

More information is available here:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Advertising

AR Measure uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its partners may collect information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • Approximate location information
  • Interaction data

You can learn more here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions Used

  • Camera permission
  • Internet permission
  • Network state permission
  • Notification permission
  • Storage permission
  • Vibration permission

These permissions are used exclusively to provide the application's features.

Local Processing

Most AR calculations are performed directly on your device.

  • Surface detection
  • Depth estimation
  • Distance calculation
  • Motion tracking
  • Screenshot generation

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only with trusted service providers such as Google AdMob and Firebase when required for application functionality.

Data Retention

Locally stored measurement information remains on your device until you remove it.

Diagnostic information collected by Firebase is retained according to Google's policies.

Children's Privacy

The application is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
