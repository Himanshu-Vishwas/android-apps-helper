1. Overview

Welcome to Arrow Out: Arrow Logic (the “Game”), published by Marsman (“Marsman”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the Game, how it is used, and what choices may be available to you.

The Game is designed as an offline-first puzzle game. Marsman does not currently provide user accounts, Firebase Authentication, cloud saves, a backend service, or developer-operated analytics for the Game.

Important: Although the core game can be played offline, advertising features provided through Google Mobile Ads may require an internet connection and may involve data processing by Google and its advertising partners.

2. Information We Handle

2.1 Information stored locally on your device

The Game may store gameplay and preference information locally on your device, such as:

  • Level progress and unlocked levels
  • Stars, scores, or best results
  • Game settings such as sound, music, and haptics preferences
  • Other local gameplay state needed to provide the Game

This information is used to operate the Game and is not transmitted to Marsman as part of the normal offline gameplay experience.

2.2 Information you provide directly

If you contact us by email, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, attachments, and any other information contained in your communication. We use that information to respond to your request, investigate problems, and provide support.

2.3 Information handled by advertising services

The Game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Depending on the version of the SDK, configuration, device settings, consent status, and ad technology in use, Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or share information used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. This may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • User interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps, and video views
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Device and account identifiers, which may include the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID where applicable

Google states that data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Google also provides users with controls for advertising identifiers and ad personalization through supported device and Google settings.

2.4 Information we do not currently request through the Game

The Game does not currently require you to provide your name, contacts, phone number, precise location, camera or microphone access, photos, or files in order to play the Game.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide banner and/or interstitial advertisements in the Game. Google may process device, advertising, diagnostic, and interaction information as described in its own documentation and privacy disclosures.

Ad behavior can depend on your device settings, applicable law, consent choices, and Google's advertising systems. We do not control the information collected or processed directly by Google through its advertising services.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure documentation

3.1 Consent for personalized advertising

Where applicable law requires consent for personalized advertising or related access to information, appropriate consent choices will be provided through the advertising consent mechanisms used by the app. You may also be able to manage advertising choices through your device or Google account settings.

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google provides specific consent requirements and tooling for AdMob. See Google's guidance for EEA, UK, and Switzerland.

4. Offline Gameplay

The core gameplay is designed to work without an internet connection. Campaign levels, gameplay logic, local progress, settings, and other game-critical functionality are intended to be available on-device.

Internet access may be used by optional third-party services such as advertising. If advertising services are unavailable because you are offline or because an ad request fails, the Game should continue to provide its core gameplay.

5. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the Game and its service providers is used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and maintaining the Game
  • Saving and restoring local gameplay progress
  • Responding to support and privacy requests
  • Displaying advertisements
  • Supporting advertising measurement, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and service operation through Google Mobile Ads
  • Complying with legal obligations and protecting the Game and its users

Marsman does not currently operate a developer-side user profiling or analytics system for the Game.

6. Sharing of Information

Marsman does not sell your personal information as a standalone business activity.

Information may nevertheless be processed by third-party service providers required for specific app functions. The main third-party service currently used for this purpose is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Google may share or disclose information within its services and with advertising partners according to its own policies, legal obligations, and the choices and settings applicable to the user.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or protect the integrity of the Game.

7. Children and Age Groups

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through our own game systems.

Because advertising services may process device and advertising information, the app's actual Google Play target-audience declaration and advertising configuration must be kept consistent with the intended audience and applicable Google Play Families requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information directly to Marsman may contact us using the address below. We will review the request and take appropriate action where required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

8.1 Local game data

Gameplay progress and settings stored locally remain on your device until they are deleted by you, the app's data is cleared, or the Game is uninstalled, subject to the behavior of the operating system and storage mechanism.

8.2 Support communications

If you contact us by email, we retain the information needed to respond to and document the request for as long as reasonably necessary, including to handle follow-up or legal obligations.

8.3 Advertising data

Data processed by Google Mobile Ads is controlled and retained by Google according to Google's policies, systems, and applicable legal requirements. Marsman does not control Google's retention periods for such information.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information, which can include rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent.

You can also control some information and advertising preferences through your device or Google account settings. Your exact options depend on your location, device, operating system, and the services involved.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle. The Game's core data is primarily stored locally on your device, and the app does not operate a developer-controlled backend for normal gameplay.

Third-party services may use their own security controls. Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

The Game may use third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Any external websites or resources linked from the Game or this Privacy Policy may be governed by policies different from ours. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of those services.
