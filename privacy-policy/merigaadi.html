Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Interpretation and Definitions

Application

"Application" refers to the Meri Gaadi mobile application.

Company

"Company", "we", "us", or "our" refers to the independent developer responsible for Meri Gaadi.

Device

"Device" means any device that can access or use the Application, including smartphones and tablets.

Personal Data

"Personal Data" means information that can be used to identify or reasonably relate to an identifiable individual.

Usage Data

"Usage Data" refers to information collected automatically through the use of the Application, such as application interactions, diagnostic information, device information, and technical data.

Information We Collect

1. Vehicle Information Saved by You

Meri Gaadi may allow you to save information about your vehicle, such as:

  • Vehicle nickname
  • Vehicle registration number
  • Document expiry dates
  • Insurance expiry information
  • PUC expiry information
  • Other vehicle-related reminder information that you choose to save
Local Storage: Vehicle information and reminder information may be stored locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account merely to use these features.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise inside the Application, information saved locally on your device is not uploaded to our servers.

2. Usage and Diagnostic Data

We may use third-party services such as Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand application usage and identify technical problems.

Such services may automatically process information including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • Application version
  • Approximate technical information
  • Application interactions
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • IP address or similar technical information where processed by the service provider

We do not intentionally send sensitive vehicle information such as vehicle registration numbers, driving licence numbers, challan numbers, OTPs, passwords, payment PINs, or other sensitive information to analytics services.

3. Advertising Data

Meri Gaadi may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, application usage information, and advertising interaction data in accordance with their applicable policies.

Advertising personalization and related controls may depend on your device settings, region, applicable laws, and Google's policies.

For more information, please review Google's privacy information: Google Privacy Policy .

How We Use Information

Information handled by the Application may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the Application.
  • To provide vehicle-related utilities and calculators.
  • To provide locally stored vehicle reminders.
  • To remember your preferences and application settings.
  • To improve application performance and usability.
  • To identify and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • To understand general application usage trends.
  • To display and measure advertisements.
  • To provide links to relevant official government services.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the Application.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Government Websites and Third-Party Services

Meri Gaadi may contain links to external websites, including official government websites.

When you open an external website, you are interacting directly with that third party. The privacy practices, data collection, cookies, authentication, payments, and terms of those websites are governed by their respective policies.

Meri Gaadi does not control how external government websites or other third-party websites process information.

For official transactions, users should carefully verify that they are visiting the genuine government website and should avoid sharing OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs, passwords, or other sensitive information with unknown persons or websites.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers used to operate and improve the Application, including:

  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Google Firebase Analytics for application analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics for crash and diagnostic reporting

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, court order, government request, or to protect the rights, security, and integrity of the Application and its users.

Data Retention

Vehicle information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the Application's data, uninstall the Application, or otherwise remove the stored information.

Analytics, advertising, and diagnostic information may be retained and processed by the respective third-party service providers in accordance with their policies and applicable legal requirements.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should also take reasonable precautions to protect your device, including using a secure screen lock and keeping your operating system and applications updated.

Children's Privacy

Meri Gaadi is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Permissions

Meri Gaadi is designed to request only permissions that are necessary for its features.

Depending on the version of the Application and enabled features, permissions may be used for functions such as notifications for vehicle document reminders.

The Application does not require access to your contacts, SMS, microphone, camera, or phone call history for its core vehicle services.

Vehicle Registration Numbers and Sensitive Information

Some features may allow you to enter vehicle-related information, such as a registration number, to help you navigate to an appropriate service.

Users should not enter passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs, CVV, banking passwords, or other confidential authentication information into Meri Gaadi unless a specific feature clearly requires it and provides appropriate security safeguards.

Meri Gaadi does not request your UPI PIN, card PIN, banking password, or OTP for ordinary vehicle-service features.

Meri Gaadi provides informational content, guides, calculators, shortcuts, and links for convenience.

Government rules, fees, fines, procedures, eligibility requirements, service availability, website addresses, and transport regulations may change without notice.

We make reasonable efforts to keep information useful and accurate, but we do not guarantee that all information is complete, current, or error-free.

For legally important or financial decisions, users should verify information through the relevant official government authority.
