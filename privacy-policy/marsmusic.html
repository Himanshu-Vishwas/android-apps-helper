Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Mars Music ("the App") is designed to provide a lightweight, fast and offline music playback experience. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data.

Information We Collect

Mars Music does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or payment information.

The App does not use analytics services, advertising services, or cloud-based tracking.

Music Library

Mars Music scans audio files stored on your device to display and play your local music collection.

Your music library information remains on your device and is never transmitted to us or stored on our servers.

Permissions

Depending on your Android version, Mars Music may request media access permissions to discover and play audio files stored on your device.

  • Photos and Videos / Music and Audio – To access and play audio files stored on your device.
  • Notifications – To display playback controls while music is playing (where required by Android).

Permissions are used only for their intended purpose and only when required for music playback.

Advertising

Mars Music does not display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

Analytics

Mars Music does not use analytics or crash reporting services.

Data Security

Your music library and playback information remain on your device. While we strive to build a secure application, no software can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Mars Music is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
